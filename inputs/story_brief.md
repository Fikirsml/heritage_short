# Story brief

Title: One Degree
Audience: family and friends
Language: English
Tone: conversational, emotional, hopeful
Visual style: cinematic realism mixed with symbolism; faceless silhouettes and POV.
Theme: faith, purpose and redirection during college.


## Audience Intent

Through a personal testimony, encourage viewers to reflect on the uncertainty of human plans and the importance of trusting God.

## Viewing Context

Family gatherings and private messages viewed on mobile phones.

## Desired Emotional Journey

Confidence → uncertainty → redirection → gratitude → hope.

## Success Criteria

- Viewers understand the central message without additional explanation.
- Viewers connect emotionally with the narrator's journey.
- The film encourages personal reflection and trust in God.

## Creative Constraints

- Preserve the original narration.
- Use cinematic realism combined with visual symbolism.
- Use faceless fictional characters, silhouettes and point-of-view shots.
- Represent God's guidance symbolically rather than physically.
- Maintain visual consistency across shots.
- Compose scenes for both horizontal and vertical formats.
