When I graduated from high school, I thought I had life figured out.

Attend classes, maybe fall in love, graduate, get a job, and life goes on. Simple, right?

Well, not quite.

I entered college without a goal or a vision. Could've at least prayed.

Year after year in civil engineering, I felt like I was drifting. In my fourth year, I made a decision I never imagined making.

I dropped out.

Engineering taught me that even a one-degree change in direction can lead to an entirely different destination.

I needed to reconsider mine.

Through prayer and reflection, I rediscovered a passion that had always been there: computers.

So I started over, this time committing my plans to God.

I felt behind, like I'd lost everything. But the story of Jesus reminds me that what appears to be defeat can end in victory.

God provided the resources to start again, friends who offered wise counsel, and encouragement along the way.

When I finally graduated in computer science, I looked back and realized that every checkpoint was an answer to prayer.

If I could tell my younger self one thing, it would be Proverbs 16:3:

“Commit thy works unto the LORD, and thy thoughts shall be established.”
